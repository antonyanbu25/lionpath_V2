© 2025 Freshworks Inc. All rights reserved.
● The ICP helps identify accounts based on fit and readiness criteria, providing
indicators of sales success and future customer satisfaction.
● The ICP is aligned to the capabilities that the Product teams design and deliver.
● The Product Marketing team creates messaging, content, and sales plays
targeting the ICP.
● The Marketing teams design and execute demand generation strategies based
on the ICP.
● The ICP includes multiple traits that can be applied to prospects and customers.
The higher the alignment of traits to a customer creates an indicator of the
customers potential to buy, and their long-term success. The higher the alignment,
the higher the potential for success. As the alignment decreases so does the
likelihood of winning a deal and the satisfaction of the customer.

Lower Priority (Proceed with Caution)
Micro-SMBs with high churn risk. These accounts require less sales focus—win rates are acceptable but retention is
challenging.
© 2025 Freshworks Inc. All rights reserved.
Company Size : 0-50 employees
Industry: Retail & ecommerce, Business & Professional Services, Manufacturing, SaaS, Fintech, Travel, Education, Online Gaming
Typical characteristics
● Small business founders or first customer service
hire setting up support
● Moving from shared inbox or no tool at all
● Ad-hoc, try-and-buy with low budget evaluations
● Often looking for free tools due to cost constraints
Selling guidance
● Do not prioritize micro-SMB leads over 50+ accounts.
These leads are inbound, but GTM priority is higher
ARPA deals with better retention.
● Quick qualification: If they have clear intent and budget,
then proceed. If they are slow growing or
budget-constrained, deprioritize to self-serve motion.
● PLG strategy in development to better serve this
segment without heavy sales involvement.
