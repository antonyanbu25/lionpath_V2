# Freshdesk Omni Ideal Customer Profile

> Summarized from Freshdesk Omni ICP/persona cards (PNG reference assets).
> Use for omnichannel motion fit scoring — not sent to model at runtime as images.

## Firmographics

- Mid-market to enterprise B2B/B2C with dedicated customer support or success org
- Typically 50–2,500+ support agents (primary SE motion: 50+ agents)
- Multi-region or multi-brand operations needing unified agent workspace
- Industries: SaaS, e-commerce/marketplace, fintech, travel/hospitality, healthcare (non-clinical support), telecom, consumer subscription

## Channel & support motion signals

- Active or planned omnichannel: email + chat + voice + WhatsApp/social messaging
- High inbound volume across channels; agents switching between tools today
- Need for unified routing, SLA management, and cross-channel customer history
- Self-service portal/KB exists but deflection rates are low or unknown
- Voice/chat queues with wait-time or abandonment pain

## AI & automation needs

- Freddy AI Agent for autonomous chat/email deflection (no-code AI Agent Studio)
- Freddy AI Copilot for agent assist (reply suggestions, summaries, tone, translation)
- Proactive insights on CSAT trends, anomalies, and staffing gaps
- Workflow automation beyond basic ticket rules (event + time triggers)

## Technology & integration context

- Incumbent may be Zendesk, Intercom, Salesforce Service Cloud, or fragmented point tools
- CRM/marketing stack integration required (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
- Marketplace/app ecosystem for custom workflows
- Cloud-only acceptable; data residency (US/EU/India/Australia) may matter

## Persona hooks (buying committee)

- VP/Director Customer Support or CX — efficiency, CSAT, cost per contact
- Head of Operations / Workforce Management — agent productivity, handle time
- IT/Application owner — integration burden, security, time-to-value
- Digital/self-service lead — deflection, KB, conversational AI ROI

## Strong fit indicators

- 50+ agents OR clear omnichannel expansion roadmap within 12 months
- Pain with tool sprawl (separate chat, voice, email systems)
- Executive mandate to reduce cost per contact while improving CSAT
- Openness to AI deflection + copilot in same platform as ticketing

## Moderate fit / probe further

- 20–49 agents with 2–3 channels but no voice yet
- Recent incumbent renewal — displacement timeline unclear
- AI initiative early stage; need education on Freddy capabilities

## Disqualifiers / weak fit

- Email-only support, &lt;15 agents, no chat/voice plans
- Pure ITSM/field service motion (Freshservice primary, not Omni)
- Hard requirement for on-prem or non-cloud deployment
- BPO-only buyer with no product ownership of stack decisions
- Budget/complexity mismatch — seeking free tier only with no growth path

## Discovery gaps to validate

- Actual agent headcount (FTE + BPO) and channel mix %
- Current stack renewal date and displacement appetite
- AI/automation maturity and prior failed bot projects
- Integration must-haves and data residency requirements
- Success metrics: CSAT, FCR, AHT, deflection rate targets
