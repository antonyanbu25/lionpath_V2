## Demo Plan

**Flow:** Confirm support model + tech-stack hypothesis (2 min) → anchor on their 24/7 B2C scale story → run 4 use cases in priority order → close with TCO comparison + reference proof.

**Use cases** *(SE to pick the Freshworks feature that best demonstrates each)*

| # | Use case | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deflect repetitive booking/billing/how-it-works queries | High-volume B2C app generates repetitive queries; travel/retail benchmarks show >50% deflection, 40–45% productivity gains |
| 2 | Unify fragmented support channels (help centre, contact form, in-app) | No single agent workspace today; consolidation cuts tool-switching and cost-to-serve |
| 3 | Fast triage & routing for time-critical incidents (accidents, breakdowns, access failures) | 24/7 safety-critical scenarios need skill-based routing + SLA enforcement |
| 4 | Faster, consistent agent responses at scale | Reduces cost-to-serve as fleet grows; multilingual SG customer base needs consistency |

**Close:** Scaling to 10K vehicles by 2030 while staying profitable — Freshworks deflects routine bookings/billing, unifies every channel, and still routes urgent incidents fast, at a lower per-agent cost than a stitched-together Zendesk suite. Propose a short pilot on their highest-volume queries.
